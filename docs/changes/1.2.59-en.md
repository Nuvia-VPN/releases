# Nuvia 1.2.59

- When less than 500 MB remains, Nuvia refreshes your subscription each time you open or return to the app, even with the VPN off.
- Fixed an issue where the notification that your traffic allowance had run out could disappear shortly after it appeared.
- Tap your remaining Free allowance on the home screen to see the plans.
- If you try to register again immediately after deleting your account, the app explains the wait. Registration resumes automatically when available.

[Download Nuvia for Android](https://github.com/Nuvia-VPN/releases/releases/download/v1.2.59/Nuvia.apk)

Install the APK over your existing version to keep your account and settings. Requires a 64-bit device with Android 7.0 or later.
